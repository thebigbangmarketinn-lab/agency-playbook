### Filling tips
- **Lead with the ROI %.** It's the first thing the owner should see.
- Always show **month-over-month change** — progress tells a story a single number can't.
- The **Speed Advantage** table is your moat made visible. Never leave it out — it's what competitors can't claim.
- If a month is weak, be honest in "What We're Improving." Owners trust agencies that own problems, and it sets up the next month's win.
