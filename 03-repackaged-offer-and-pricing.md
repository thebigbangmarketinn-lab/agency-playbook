# Deliverable 3 — Repackaged Offer & Pricing Tiers

**Goal:** Move from "₹15k to run your ads" to **₹40k+ for a customer-acquisition system** — using Deliverables 1 & 2 as the value you're now charging for.

---

## 1. The positioning shift

| OLD (commodity) | NEW (premium) |
|-----------------|---------------|
| "We run your Facebook & Instagram ads" | "We build you a customer-acquisition system" |
| You deliver: leads | You deliver: **booked, qualified customers** |
| You report: clicks, ROAS | You report: **cost per customer, revenue, ROI** |
| Easy to compare on price | Hard to compare — nobody else offers the full system |
| Client thinks: expense | Client thinks: investment with a known return |

**Your new one-liner:**
> "We don't just get you leads — we get you customers. Ads bring people in, our WhatsApp system follows up in 60 seconds and qualifies them, and you get a monthly report showing exactly how many paying customers we delivered and what each one cost."

---

## 2. The three tiers

### 🥉 STARTER — ₹25,000/month
*For small/local businesses, low ad budgets.*
- Meta (FB + Instagram) ad management — up to ₹50k/mo ad spend
- 1 campaign objective (lead gen OR sales)
- 6–8 creatives/month (edited from client-supplied raw footage)
- **WhatsApp speed-to-lead automation** (instant first response)
- Basic lead tracking sheet
- Monthly performance report
- *Setup fee: ₹15,000 one-time (WhatsApp + tracking setup)*

### 🥈 GROWTH — ₹45,000/month  ⭐ *most popular — anchor here*
*For businesses ready to scale.*
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Ad spend managed up to ₹2L/mo
- Multiple campaigns + A/B testing
- 12–16 creatives/month + hook-testing library
- **Full WhatsApp system:** speed-to-lead + qualification + nurture + appointment reminders + monthly cold-lead re-engagement
- **Closed-loop cost-per-customer reporting** (CAC, ROAS, ROI)
- Landing page / offer optimization (1 page)
- Bi-weekly strategy call
- *Setup fee: ₹20,000 one-time*

### 🥇 SCALE / PARTNER — ₹75,000+/month
*For established businesses treating you as their growth partner.*
- Everything in Growth, plus:
- Ad spend managed ₹2L+/mo
- Dedicated account manager
- 20+ creatives/month + UGC creator coordination
- Multi-channel (Meta + Google + optional YouTube)
- Full CRM setup + sales-team WhatsApp inbox training
- Weekly reporting + monthly business review with owner
- Offer & pricing strategy consulting
- *Setup fee: ₹35,000 one-time*

> **Pricing psychology:** The Starter exists to make Growth look like the obvious choice (the anchor). Most clients should land on **Growth at ₹45k**. Scale exists to make Growth feel affordable and to capture your biggest clients. Never lead with the cheapest option.

## 4. Migrating your 6 existing clients (the script)

Don't just announce a price hike. **Deliver the new value first, then reprice.** Sequence:

**Step 1 — Roll out the WhatsApp system + new report to your best client FIRST (free, as a "beta").** Get 30 days of results.

**Step 2 — Show the result, then have this conversation:**

> "Hey {Name}, over the last month we changed how we work with you. Every lead now gets a WhatsApp reply within a minute, gets qualified automatically, and we follow up until they book. Here's the report — instead of just leads, this shows we got you **{X} actual customers** at **₹{Y} each**, which means **₹{Z} in revenue** from your spend.
>
> This is a different level of service than the basic ad management we started with. We're moving everyone to our new customer-acquisition packages. For what you're getting, that's the **Growth plan at ₹45,000/month**. Given you've been with us from the start, I can hold your onboarding fee and start you at **₹38,000/month** for the first 3 months — does that work?"

**Why it works:**
- You show **proof of new value** before asking for more money.
- You **anchor at ₹45k**, then "discount" to ₹38k — feels like a win for them, still a 2x+ increase for you.
- Framing it as "everyone is moving" removes the personal negotiation.

**Step 3 — Expect 1–2 to push back.** That's fine. Even if you lose one, replacing a ₹15k client with a ₹40k+ client at the new positioning nets you ahead. Math: 6 clients × ₹17k avg = **₹1.02L/mo** today → even 5 clients × ₹40k = **₹2L/mo**, nearly double with *fewer* clients to service.

---

## 5. Selling the new offer to NEW clients

Lead the sales conversation with the **outcome and the gap**, not the deliverables:

1. *"How fast does your team currently follow up with a new enquiry?"* (Answer is always "a few hours / next day.")
2. *"Most businesses lose the majority of leads simply because nobody replies fast enough. We fix that — every lead gets a WhatsApp reply in under a minute, automatically."*
3. *"And instead of a confusing report full of clicks, you'll see exactly how many customers you got and what each cost."*
4. Present **Growth (₹45k)** as the default. Offer Starter only if budget is genuinely tight.

---

## 6. 90-day execution plan (ties all 3 deliverables together)

| Phase | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Week 1–2 | Set up WhatsApp flow (Deliverable 1) + tracking sheet (Deliverable 2) on your **best client** |
| Week 3–4 | Collect results, build the one-page report, capture the win |
| Week 5–8 | Reprice that client using the script → roll out system to remaining 5 |
| Week 9–12 | Reprice the rest; launch the new 3-tier offer for all NEW prospects |
| Outcome | Fewer-or-same clients, ~2x revenue, a service competitors can't easily copy |
